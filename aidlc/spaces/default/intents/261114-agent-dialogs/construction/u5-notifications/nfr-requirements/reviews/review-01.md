### Summary

The security rows are specific and testable, and the narrowing of the permission body to `what` is justified by C3's field definitions; it only needs its D3 and D9 knock-on changes written down. The one real gap is that NFR3.51 misses LRM, RLM, U+061C and zero-width characters, so its "no direction characters" target is not met by its own rule. The remaining items are minor tightening of bounds, the decided-id reasoning for the audit path, the 1 s measurement's scope, overdue-state accessibility and the order of the C4 change.
