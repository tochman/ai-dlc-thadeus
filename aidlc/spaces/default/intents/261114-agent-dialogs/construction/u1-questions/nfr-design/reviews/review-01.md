### Summary

The design is mostly sound. The 595 s deadline is correct against the 600 s timeout (exit between 594 s and 596.1 s). The heartbeat in a worker thread does not stall. The wait marker, take-by-rename and unlink tie-breaker keep a late answer from being reported as sent via hook in the cases that were designed for. Secret handling and `0700` folders are adequate. Two points should be settled before code: the dash redirect bug in the hook script, and the start-up wipe against a second app instance. The rest is wording or small hardening.
