### Summary

The design closes the earlier review's points well. Fixed paths, `lstat` on every part, a synchronous `applying` state, exact IPC argument lists, and the order of calls around the final compare all hold up against `fileWrite.js`, `projectAccess.js` and `watcher.js`. The watcher widening to `.sh` and `.js` is correct, since `isFile` today accepts only `.md`, `.json` and dot-named files. The two things to fix before Code Generation are the unanchored `appKind` regex, which breaks the "exactly" ownership promise and hides behind the post-check, and the post-check's weak proof that nothing outside the app's spans changed. Neither is Critical, so the verdict is READY.
