### Summary

The security and reliability design is sound on its main points: atomic temp-and-rename writes, a safe file order, the guard in each command, the refusal on doubt, and a clear ban on loosening `isEngineOwned`. Two Major points need fixing before Code Generation. One is the contradictory rule for which hooks are the app's, which can delete a teammate's hook. The other is the three gaps in the `JSON.parse` post-check: duplicate keys, the BOM, and no canonical check of the result. The Minor points tighten the preview-to-write window, symlink handling and round-trip claims. The verdict is READY because there are no Critical findings and no more than two Major ones, but I recommend fixing both Major ones.
