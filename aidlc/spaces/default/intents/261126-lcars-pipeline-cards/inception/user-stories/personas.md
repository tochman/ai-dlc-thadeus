# Personas — US-0412 LCARS pipeline cards

## P1 Dana, the developer following a workflow (primary)

- **Role:** runs AI-DLC workflows in THADEUS and keeps the Pipeline view open beside the agent terminal.
- **Goals:** see at a glance which stage is running, which needs them, and what just finished; feel they are at a living LCARS console.
- **Pain points:** US-0411's cards look flat; a stage that changes or finishes is easy to miss.
- **Tech comfort:** high. **Frequency:** daily, for hours at a time.

## P2 Robin, the person who turned motion down (secondary)

- **Role:** the same kind of user, with the animations setting on Reduced or Off (motion sensitivity, focus, or a slow machine).
- **Goals:** the same information as Dana, with no movement they did not ask for.
- **Pain points:** flashing or sliding effects that ignore the setting.
- **Tech comfort:** high. **Frequency:** daily.

## Relationship and priority

Dana is who the effects are for; Robin sets the limit they must respect. A story that pleases Dana but moves for Robin is not done.
