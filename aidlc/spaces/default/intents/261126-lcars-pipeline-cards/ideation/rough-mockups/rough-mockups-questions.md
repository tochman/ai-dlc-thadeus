# Rough Mockups — Questions (US-0412)

Context: today each stage card (US-0411) has a thin head strip with the stage name, a black body with "NN · Status", and a foot with agent, duration and the level bar. Only the current stage breathes and "needs you" pulses coral. The animations setting (Full / Reduced / Off) already turns motion off app-wide.

## Q1. [Design] How much more LCARS should the card shape get?
Why: this decides how far the cards change from US-0411's version.

A. Bolder head strip: taller, a rounded end cap, and the stage number in its own colour block on the left
B. Elbow: the head strip bends down the card's left edge like an LCARS elbow, the number sits in the elbow
C. Both: elbow with a number block, plus the foot split into small segment blocks
D. Keep the shape, change only colour and motion
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: B

## Q2. [Design] Which animations do you want? (select all that apply)
Why: the story names three; each runs only with animations on Full.

A. Power-up when the pipeline opens: the cards light up one after another, left to right, in about half a second
B. Sweep when a stage changes state: a band of light passes across that one card once
C. Flash when a stage completes: the card flashes twice (well under 3 a second) and settles to done
D. Level bar: a new lit segment fills in when a unit is approved
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A, B, C, D
