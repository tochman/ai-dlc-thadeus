## Build order

The order follows the theme: each story should make the ones after it cheaper or safer to build.

| # | Story | AI-DLC plan | Why here |
|---|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | **US-0212** rule pack | ultra-express | Once it is on in this project, every later intent runs with it, so it saves tokens on the rest of the release. |
| 2 | **US-0211** repricing | ultra-express | Correct figures before we measure what the later stories save. Small and contained (`src/main/parsers/`). |
| 3 | **US-0606** planning commits | express | Git worktrees and the checkout must stay untouched: worth a design step. |
| 4 | **US-0607** local record and push | express | Builds on 3. From here on, release planning cannot be lost. |
| 5 | **US-0213** compact or fresh session | express | Largest story. Uses the compact instruction and settings from 1; its open questions (a gate in a fresh session, autonomous Construction) go to its first design stage. |
| 6 | **US-0210** model profile | ultra-express | Judged with the right prices (2) and the savings from 1 and 5 in view. |

**Working rules for the release:**
- One story at a time. Two stories from the same `main` mean merging `main` in and running the whole suite again (project rule), which is waste. The exception: 2 and 3 touch different code and may run side by side if time matters more than tokens.
- A fresh session after each stage. During the TDD loop run only the test being worked on; the full suite runs once before Build and Test is approved, and in CI.
