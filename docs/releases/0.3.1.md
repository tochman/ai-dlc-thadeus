# Release plan v0.3.1

> Moved from v0.3.0 on 2026-11-19 at the person's request: v0.3.0 is the Spec Kit release (EPIC-14). Later releases follow as v0.3.2, v0.3.3 and so on. EPIC-13 (US-1301 to US-1307) moved here from v1.0.0 the same day; the product is not ready for 1.0. The release then grew too large and was split by topic and value: US-0412, US-0806, US-1102 and US-1304 to US-1307 moved to [v0.3.2](0.3.2.md). US-0609 moved to [v0.2.13](0.2.13.md) on 2026-11-20 at the person's request.

**Theme:** Steer the work, and a livelier console: change a started intent, archive finished work, tool sign-in and a health check, and motion in every view and on the pipeline cards.

**Scope change (2026-11-25):** Rebalanced with v0.3.2 and v0.3.3 at the person's request. US-1301 to US-1303 moved to [v0.3.2](0.3.2.md), and US-0412 moved here from there, next to US-0050.
